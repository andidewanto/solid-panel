# Solid Panel

**Turn a flat shape or a few lines into a solid panel.**

Free · Made on SketchUp 2026 for macOS · Version 1.2.1

[**Download**](https://andidewanto.github.io/solid-panel/) · [**Tutorial**](https://andidewanto.github.io/solid-panel/tutorial.html)

---

**From a flat shape:** a face, or a group or component whose faces lie in one plane, becomes a panel of the thickness you choose. Holes are filled.

**From lines:** connected lines, open or closed, straight or arcs, are given a thickness and pulled to the length you choose.

**Copy as Vector (macOS):** select faces, right-click, **Copy as Vector**, and paste into Illustrator at 1:1 in mm, with holes and real curves.

Sharp bends stay sharp and only real curves are smoothed. The **Smooth result** checkbox turns smoothing off. The panel is always a new group, and one Undo removes it.

## Bahasa Indonesia

**Ubah bentuk datar atau beberapa garis menjadi panel solid.**

**Dari bentuk datar:** face, atau grup atau komponen yang face-nya sebidang, menjadi panel setebal yang Anda pilih. Lubang ditutup.

**Dari garis:** garis yang tersambung, terbuka atau tertutup, lurus atau arc, diberi ketebalan dan ditarik sepanjang yang Anda pilih.

**Copy as Vector (macOS):** pilih face, klik kanan, **Copy as Vector**, lalu paste ke Illustrator dengan skala 1:1 dalam mm, lengkap dengan lubang dan kurva sungguhan.

Tekukan tetap tajam dan hanya lengkungan sungguhan yang dihaluskan. Checkbox **Smooth result** mematikan penghalusan. Panel selalu berupa grup baru, dan satu kali Undo menghapusnya.
